Meet Me

Amee Byers

Founder of Shanti Salon (est. 2013), Amee Byers trained at Toni & Guy and spent nine years in top Arizona salons before creating a more personal, one-on-one experience. She opened one of Northern Arizona’s first single-studio salons, keeping Flagstaff looking and feeling its best. Outside the salon, Amee cherishes time with her husband and two boys, supports local businesses, and enjoys gardening, fitness, and the great outdoors.
